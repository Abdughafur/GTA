# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Added a debounced, versioned browser save for player position, funds, needs, appearance, world conditions, vehicle customization, settings, and transactions.
- Restored the saved player position when creating a game session.
- Changed simulation updates to a 60 Hz fixed step with a bounded catch-up budget.
- Added an Ultra graphics preset and applied quality changes to render resolution and shadows.
- Expanded the central plaza with a fountain monument, seating, planters, and night lamps.
- Added a fenced farm with crop rows, a barn, a silo, and wandering cow/sheep rigs.
- Added six optional character-led missions accessible through the phone's message inbox.
- Expanded the story to eight sequential chapters, with package pickup/drop-off objectives, a faction choice, saved reputation, driving skill, and achievements.
- Added time- and district-adjusted mission payouts, a purchasable starter studio with rest, and paid turbo/underglow upgrades that affect the active car.
- Added class-specific vehicle mass, grip, and suspension settings; front-wheel steering pivots, independent wheel spin/suspension response; tire temperature/wear/impact damage; ABS/ESP assists; and interpolated vehicle rendering.
- Added batched reflective lane markings, two-axis synchronized traffic signals with AI stop behavior, rush-hour traffic pacing, and a scheduled spline-route city bus with four shelters; retained the existing spline-loop metro.
- Removed the unused Gemini SDK and AI Studio setup from the app dependencies and project instructions.
